# Interview Preparation Guide

## STAR Format
Structure answers as Situation, Task, Action, Result. Keep answers to 1-2 minutes and validate any missing context before using a draft as a final interview story.

## Common Tough Questions

### "Why are you looking for a new role?"
Prepare a forward-looking answer focused on engineering leadership, architecture, global remote-first opportunities, and CTO-track growth. Do not criticize current or former employers.

### "You don't have AI/ML experience."
Be direct that specific AI/ML frameworks are not documented in the current profile. Bridge to transferable experience in platform architecture, production reliability, APIs, engineering leadership, and delivery under operational constraints.

### "Why this company specifically?"
Customize per company using independently verified company facts and the role's actual technical and business challenges.

### "What is your biggest weakness?"
The supplied documents do not provide a confirmed answer. Ask the candidate before drafting one.

## Questions to Ask Interviewers
- What would success look like in the first six months?
- Which reliability, delivery, or architecture challenges are most urgent?
- How much authority does this role have over technical direction and team practices?
- How does the company support remote-first collaboration across time zones?
- If relocation is required, what relocation support and package are provided?
