# CV Templates and Tailoring Guide

Use the moderncv banking template, compile with `lualatex`, and keep targeted CVs to exactly two pages. All factual claims must be grounded in `01-candidate-profile.md`.

## Candidate profile statement templates

**For engineering leadership roles:**
> Engineering leader with 13+ years of software engineering experience, including leadership of fintech and SaaS teams. Experienced in payment infrastructure, banking APIs, KYC, platform reliability, developer productivity, and cloud-cost optimization. Raised platform SLO from 98.7% to 99.9%, improved DORA metrics by 70%, and reduced cloud costs by approximately 40%. Seeking engineering leadership roles where technical direction and team development improve dependable product delivery.

**For staff, architecture, and senior individual-contributor roles:**
> Senior software engineer and technical leader with 13+ years of experience designing and delivering payment, banking, SaaS, and API platforms. Brings hands-on experience with Go, Java, TypeScript, system architecture, performance optimization, observability, and production reliability. Led platform rewrites and integrations across payment gateways and banking APIs, while partnering with product and business teams to deliver measurable outcomes.

**For forward-deployed or AI-native leadership roles:**
> Engineering leader with 13+ years of experience translating complex operational and financial workflows into reliable software platforms. Brings a background in payments, banking APIs, KYC, SaaS, cross-functional delivery, and leading teams through high-stakes production work. Position AI-native or forward-deployed claims only where the target role's responsibilities match the candidate's confirmed systems, customer, and stakeholder experience; no specific AI/ML framework experience is currently documented.

## Tailoring rules
- Lead with the role's actual leadership, architecture, reliability, platform, or stakeholder requirements.
- Preserve the candidate's measured outcomes: 99.9% SLO, 70% DORA improvement, 40% cloud-cost reduction, 50% resource reduction, 30% API response-time improvement, and 4-second page-load improvement.
- Do not add AI/ML frameworks, US/EU work authorization, or technologies not present in the candidate profile without confirmation.
- Use the supplied English CV as the default language.
- Compile targeted CVs with `lualatex`; verify exactly two pages and ATS text extraction before delivery.
