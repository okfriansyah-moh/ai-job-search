# Job Evaluation Framework

## Candidate-specific match areas

**Strong match areas:** Engineering management, software engineering leadership, payment infrastructure, banking APIs, SaaS platforms, Go, Java, TypeScript, system and architecture design, DORA metrics, SLOs, cloud-cost optimization, APIs, KYC, compliance, Agile/Scrum, OKRs, Docker, Jenkins, Grafana, MySQL, PostgreSQL.

**Moderate match areas:** Staff/principal engineering, software architecture, CTO-track roles, developer productivity, platform engineering, technical program leadership, Node.js, Angular, Spring Boot, Alibaba Cloud, Swift.

**Weak or unverified areas:** Specific AI/ML frameworks, production machine-learning systems, named US/EU work authorization, and any role-specific technology not listed in the candidate profile.

## Eligibility Gate
For roles outside Indonesia, verify citizenship, work authorization, sponsorship, and relocation support from the employer. Remote work is required. An onsite or hybrid role outside Indonesia is eligible only when the posting explicitly confirms employer-sponsored relocation/support and a relocation package. A silent posting is unverified and must be treated as ineligible for the location gate. Indonesia-based roles may be onsite, hybrid, or remote.

## Language Gate
| Language | Declared level |
|----------|----------------|
| Indonesian | Native |
| English | Professional working proficiency |

Exclude roles requiring an undeclared language as a job condition. Flag roles requiring a higher English level than professional working proficiency for the candidate's judgment.

## Scoring Dimensions

Evaluate technical skills, experience, behavioral/culture fit, location/logistics, and career alignment using the scoring tables and weighting below. Location is pass/fail only when the employer offers neither remote work nor supported relocation.

### Career alignment
**Target roles:** Engineering Manager, Director of Engineering, Staff Engineer, CTO, Software Architect, Solution Architect, Solution Lead Engineer, Lead Engineer, Engineering Lead, Team Lead, Lead Software Engineer, Senior Software Engineer, Software Engineer II, AI Engineer, AI-native Lead, AI Enablement Lead, Automation AI Engineer, Forward Deployed Engineer, Forward Deployed Engineer Lead, Senior Forward Deployed Engineer, Forward Deployed Manager, Software Engineering Manager, IT Manager, Platform Engineer, Backend Engineer, Developer Productivity Engineer, and other related engineering roles that match the candidate's leadership, architecture, payments, platform, API, reliability, or systems background.

**Career goals:**
- Progress toward senior engineering leadership, software architecture, and CTO-track responsibility.
- Lead teams and systems that improve reliability, developer productivity, cost efficiency, and business outcomes.
- Explore AI-native and forward-deployed engineering leadership roles where the candidate's systems and stakeholder background transfers credibly. AI/ML depth must be verified per role.
- Prioritize roles with strong compensation or transparent salary ranges, while evaluating compensation against seniority, scope, location, and the candidate's experience.

**Geographic exclusion:** Exclude India-based roles, roles requiring the candidate to work from India, and India-based employers unless the candidate explicitly requests an exception. Remote roles for employers based elsewhere remain eligible.

**Motivation filter:**
- **Tasks that energize, inferred from supplied documents:** Technical direction, architecture, reliability, team development, platform modernization, cross-functional problem solving, and measurable engineering improvement.
- **Tasks to validate:** Work dominated by routine maintenance, little technical influence, or no team/product ownership.
- **Non-task factors:** Remote-first culture, autonomy, strategic engineering influence, and practical support for relocation where office presence is required.

## Location & Logistics
- **Preferred:** US remote, South Korea, Japan, Germany, the Netherlands, Europe, Slovakia, Balkan territories, Singapore, Qatar, the UAE, Arab countries, Asia, and remote roles worldwide.
- **Remote:** Required for every role outside Indonesia, with global time-zone compatibility checked per posting.
- **Relocation:** Exception only when employer-sponsored relocation/support and a relocation package are explicitly stated. Relocation without both forms of support is not acceptable.
- **Exclude:** India-based roles, India-based employers, or roles requiring work from India unless explicitly requested.
- **Frequent international travel:** Flag for discussion.

## Weighting
- Technical Skills: 30%
- Experience Match: 25%
- Behavioral Fit: 15%
- Career Alignment: 30%

Location and eligibility are gates, not weighted dimensions.

## Thresholds
- **Strong Fit** (75+): Definitely apply, tailor everything
- **Good Fit** (60-74): Apply, address gaps in cover letter
- **Moderate Fit** (45-59): Consider carefully, discuss with user
- **Weak Fit** (30-44): Probably skip unless strategic reasons
- **Poor Fit** (<30): Skip

Use the full eligibility, language, scoring, salary, research, and output procedures in this file's original workflow when evaluating a posting.
